# KiCadD tools: the full descriptions

Generated by `kicadd-mcp --tool-docs` (do not edit: the text lives with each tool in mcp/*.cpp). The MCP tools/list sends only the first line of each; `tool_help {name}` returns the rest.

## board_summary

Counts, copper layers and outline of the open PCB (mm, Y down). Start here.

Counts, layer count and board outline of the open PCB. Units: mm. KiCad's Y axis points down.

## query_area

Items near a part or a point, nearest first. Use instead of dumping the board.

Items near a footprint (ref: the part itself first, then what is around it) or a point (x,y), nearest first. Lines: KIND handle [net] layer @x,y (pads are REF.PADNUM; tracks start>end wWIDTH; ~DIST from the centre). Use this instead of dumping the board.

Parameter notes:

- kinds: Item kinds to include (default: footprint, pad, track, arc, via)

Arguments:

- `kinds` (array): default footprint, pad, track, arc, via
- `limit` (integer): Max items (default 64)
- `radius` (number): Radius, mm
- `ref` (string): Centre on this footprint
- `x` (number): Centre X, mm
- `y` (number): Centre Y, mm

## query_net

Pads, tracks, vias and zones of one net.

Pads, tracks, vias and zones on one net (topology of a single net).

Arguments:

- `limit` (integer): Max items (default 256)
- `net` (string): Net name, e.g. GND or /USB/D+

## move_footprints

Move parts by a delta or to absolute places (x, y, rotation, side), one undoable edit; returns a local DRC around them.

Move/rotate/flip footprints as one undoable edit: refs with dx / dy / rotate / flip (move, then rotate, then flip; each around its own anchor, or as a group with pivot=group), or parts {"U1": {"x": 105, "y": 120, "rotation": 90, "side": "bottom"}} to put each where it is to be (absolute: anchor mm, orientation as KiCad shows it, side top / bottom; what is left out stays). Returns new positions (B.Cu = bottom side), the unrouted-connection count and a local DRC of the moved parts (clearance, shorts, hole clearance, courtyard overlap, board-edge clearance, copper outside the board). Large nets (GND, power) skip ratsnest updates while you iterate; the count says which.

Parameter notes:

- flip: Move to the other side (F.Cu <-> B.Cu), mirrored left-right
- pivot: Rotate/flip each part around its own anchor (default) or the parts as one group around their common centre

Arguments:

- `detail` (boolean): list everything (default: long lists cut short)
- `dx` (number): Delta X, mm
- `dy` (number): Delta Y, mm (down is +)
- `flip` (boolean): to the other side (mirrored)
- `parts` (object): {ref: {x, y, rotation, side}}, absolute
- `pivot` (string): each part around itself (default), or the group
- `refs` (array): Reference designators, e.g. ["U5", "C12"]
- `rotate` (number): Rotation delta, degrees CCW

## lcsc_fetch

Get an LCSC part's footprint, symbol and 3D model into the project libraries, with stock and price.

Get the footprint and schematic symbol of an LCSC part (e.g. C8734) from the JLCEDA/EasyEDA Official Library: converted to KiCad, saved to the project library LCSC (lcsc.pretty + lcsc.kicad_sym, added to fp-lib-table and sym-lib-table; the symbol's Footprint field points at the footprint), cached for offline use. Returns both lib_ids, size, pad numbers/positions and any pin/pad mismatch. Its 3D model (EasyEDA's, converted to VRML in lcsc.3dshapes/, placed as EasyEDA places it) comes along for the 3D viewer unless model=false. add_footprint with lcsc does this by itself.

Arguments:

- `lcsc` (string): LCSC part number, e.g. C8734
- `model` (boolean): the 3D model too (default true)
- `refresh` (boolean): Fetch again even if cached

## add_footprint

Place a new footprint (library, LCSC number, or a copy of a part) with its pad nets, as one undoable edit.

Place a new footprint as one undoable edit: from a library (footprint = "Library:Name", e.g. "Capacitor_SMD:C_0603_1608Metric"), from an LCSC part number (lcsc = "C8734", fetched and saved to the project library), or as a copy of a part already on the board (like = "C12"). ref "C?" takes the next free number. nets maps pad numbers to nets; unknown nets are created and reported. Returns the placement, unrouted count and local DRC.

Parameter notes:

- lcsc: LCSC part number, e.g. C8734 (instead of footprint)
- nets: Pad number -> net name, e.g. {"1": "+3V3", "2": "GND"}
- ref: Reference, e.g. C301 or C? (default: prefix of like)

Arguments:

- `back` (boolean): Place on the bottom side
- `detail` (boolean): list everything (default: long lists cut short)
- `footprint` (string): Library:Name
- `lcsc` (string): LCSC number, e.g. C8734
- `like` (string): Copy the footprint of this ref instead
- `nets` (object): {pad: net}
- `ref` (string): e.g. C301, or C? for the next free
- `rotate` (number): Orientation, degrees CCW
- `value` (string): Value, e.g. 100nF
- `x` (number): Position X, mm
- `y` (number): Position Y, mm

## delete_footprints

Delete parts as one undoable edit (tracks stay).

Delete footprints as one undoable edit (undo restores them with their nets). Tracks and vias stay; a net nothing else is on goes with them. Returns the new unrouted count.

Arguments:

- `detail` (boolean): list everything (default: long lists cut short)
- `refs` (array): Reference designators, e.g. ["U5", "C12"]

## delete_tracks

Delete tracks / vias (all, by net, by part, or in an area), as one undoable edit.

Delete routing (tracks, arcs, vias) as one undoable edit, e.g. before auto_place on a routed board. all: every track; nets: by net name; refs: the copper running from these parts' pads up to the next pad (rip up their connections); region: [x0, y0, x1, y1] mm, anything crossing it. Given together, nets / refs / region narrow each other (GND in this box). The answer lists what went per net. Locked tracks stay unless include_locked. Zones are not touched (refill_zones afterwards). Returns the new unrouted count.

Arguments:

- `all` (boolean)
- `detail` (boolean): list everything (default: long lists cut short)
- `include_locked` (boolean)
- `keep_vias` (boolean)
- `nets` (array): Net names, e.g. ["GND", "/USB_D+"]
- `refs` (array): Reference designators, e.g. ["U5", "C12"]
- `region` (array)

## route

Draw one connection like the interactive router (to a pad, a point, or the net's pour). Not an autorouter.

Draw one connection the way the interactive router does (45-degree tracks around obstacles; existing copper is never moved; the net class's width and via, the clearance, keepout and edge rules). Not an autorouter: say what to connect. from: a pad "U1.5" or a point [x, y] on the net's pad / track / via (inside a pad: the track leaves the pad there, for a way out between close pads); to: likewise, or any point (a free end), or "zone": the net's own pour, its nearest filled copper (on the start's layer, else with a via down to the nearest layer that has it: a pad to a VBUS / GND pour); without to: the nearest unrouted connection of from. waypoints: [[x, y], ...] to pass through, [x, y, "B.Cu"] puts a via there and goes on on that layer; via: [x, y] is short for one via to the other side (the end's layer). layer: where to start (default: the start pad's / the end's / F.Cu); width in mm. Reports length vs the straight line, segments, vias and the narrowest gap; when blocked, how far it got and what is in the way. One undoable edit; dry_run reports without drawing. Mm, Y down.

Parameter notes:

- from: "U1.5" or [x, y] (a point inside a pad: the track leaves the pad there, not at its centre)

Arguments:

- `dry_run` (boolean)
- `from`: "U1.5" or [x, y]
- `layer` (string)
- `to`: "C3.1", [x, y], or "zone" (the net's pour); omit: ratsnest
- `via` (array)
- `waypoints` (array)
- `width` (number)

## route_short

Route the shortest unrouted connections of given parts / nets, one by one as route does.

Route the unrouted connections (ratsnest lines) of refs and / or nets, shortest first (the short ones have the fewest ways round, so they go first and block the least), each as route does; max_length (mm) leaves the long ones to the user. Through-hole to through-hole: F.Cu, then B.Cu if blocked, unless layer is given; when the nearest copper of the other end can't be reached (a track on the far layer), the pads and vias joined to it, nearest first. No vias are added. The failures say how far they got and what is in the way (delete_tracks or move the part). All of it is one undoable edit; dry_run reports what would and would not route without drawing.

Arguments:

- `detail` (boolean): list everything (default: long lists cut short)
- `dry_run` (boolean)
- `layer` (string)
- `max_length` (number)
- `nets` (array): Net names, e.g. ["GND", "/USB_D+"]
- `refs` (array): Reference designators, e.g. ["U5", "C12"]

## fanout

Short track + via from SMD pads to a pour of the same net on another layer (GND / power fan-out).

Fan out SMD pads of nets (e.g. GND, +3V3) to a copper zone of the same net on another layer (an inner plane, or the other side of a 2-layer board): a short track and a via next to each pad, inside that zone, as route would draw them. Through-hole pads are skipped (they reach every layer), and so are pads already joined to the zone; a net without such a zone gets a warning, not dangling vias. refs: only these parts; layer: the zone's layer. One undoable edit; the zones are refilled afterwards (refill=false to skip); dry_run reports without drawing.

Arguments:

- `detail` (boolean): list everything (default: long lists cut short)
- `dry_run` (boolean)
- `layer` (string)
- `nets` (array): Net names, e.g. ["GND", "/USB_D+"]
- `refill` (boolean)
- `refs` (array): Reference designators, e.g. ["U5", "C12"]

## stitch_vias

Stitching vias on a grid over a net's pours (default GND), plus one per unconnected island.

Stitching vias for a net's pours (default GND): through vias on a grid (spacing, default 5 mm; area [x0, y0, x1, y1] mm, default the whole pour), each inside the net's filled copper on two or more layers, clear of pads, tracks, holes, the edge and rule areas, and outside the parts' courtyards (under_parts=true allows them there). Then one via in each island of the net's copper that has none (islands=false skips it). Net class via size. The zones are refilled before and after; one undoable edit; dry_run counts without adding.

Arguments:

- `area` (array)
- `dry_run` (boolean)
- `islands` (boolean)
- `max` (integer)
- `net` (string)
- `refill` (boolean)
- `spacing` (number): mm
- `under_parts` (boolean)

## teardrops

Add or remove KiCad teardrops on vias and pads.

Teardrops (KiCad's own: copper that widens a track into its pad or via) on every via and through-hole pad, or remove=true to take them off. smd=true adds SMD pads; vias=false / pth=false leave those out; nets / refs narrow it (refs alone: those parts' pads only). They follow later edits as in the editor. One undoable edit; the zones are refilled.

Arguments:

- `dry_run` (boolean)
- `nets` (array): Net names, e.g. ["GND", "/USB_D+"]
- `pth` (boolean)
- `refs` (array): Reference designators, e.g. ["U5", "C12"]
- `remove` (boolean)
- `smd` (boolean)
- `vias` (boolean)

## get_rules

Net classes, their net patterns and the board's minimum constraints.

The design rules: net classes (clearance, track width, via, diff pair; unset values come from Default) with how many nets each has, the net class assignments by net name pattern, and the board's minimum constraints. Lengths in mm.

## set_rules

Change net classes (width, clearance, via, diff pair, current, impedance), assignments or fab presets, undoable.

Change the design rules as one undoable edit (undo / redo; save_board writes them to the project file). classes: {"Power": {"track_width": 0.5, "clearance": 0.25}} adds or changes a class (only the values given; null: take it from Default); {"Power": {"current": 2}} sets the track width a 2 A net needs (IPC-2221, the board's outer copper, temp_rise 10 C unless given); {"RF": {"impedance": 50}} sets the track width for 50 ohm and {"USB": {"diff_impedance": 90}} the diff pair width and gap for 90 ohm, from the board's stackup on impedance_layer (default F.Cu; board_setup stackup=... first for a fab's numbers): the pair's width at diff_pair_gap if given, its gap at diff_pair_width if given, else its width with the class's clearance as the gap; the note gives the widths on the other layers and warns of reference layers not poured yet; remove_classes: [...]; assign: {"+5V": "Power", "/USB_D*": "USB", "GND": null} sets or drops net name patterns; constraints: {"min_clearance", "min_track_width", "min_via_diameter", "min_through_drill", "min_annular_width", "hole_clearance", "hole_to_hole", "copper_edge_clearance", "min_silk_text_height", "min_silk_text_thickness"} (mm). preset: a fab's standard process as the board minimums (and the Default class raised to them): jlcpcb_2layer, jlcpcb_4layer, jlcpcb_6layer; the rest of the call goes on top. Existing tracks keep their width. drc (default true) runs DRC before and after and reports the change in errors.

Arguments:

- `assign` (object)
- `classes` (object)
- `constraints` (object)
- `drc` (boolean)
- `preset` (string)
- `remove_classes` (array): Net class names to remove, e.g. ["USB"]

## zones

List, create or change copper pours.

Copper zones (pours): list, create, or change them. No arguments: the list (id, net, layers, priority, clearance, minimum width, pad connection). create: a new zone, e.g. {"create": {"net": "GND"}} pours GND on F.Cu and B.Cu over the whole board; {"create": {"net": "GND", "layers": ["In1.Cu"]}} makes an inner plane; rect [x0, y0, x1, y1] or outline [[x, y], ...] (mm) for a part of the board. Where zones of different nets overlap, the higher priority is filled and the other keeps clear: a new zone inside a bigger one is put above it, and an overlap at the same priority is warned about. Change settings: choose with nets, layers, ids (from the list) or all=true, and give clearance, min_width (mm), priority, connection (solid / thermal / none / thermal_tht), thermal_gap, thermal_spoke (mm). Each is one undoable edit, refilled afterwards unless refill=false.

Parameter notes:

- create: a new zone (not together with nets / layers / ids / all)
- create.layers: default ["F.Cu", "B.Cu"]; ["all"]: every copper layer

Arguments:

- `all` (boolean)
- `clearance` (number)
- `connection` (string)
- `create` (object): a new zone
- `ids` (array): Zone ids from the list
- `layers` (array): Layer names, e.g. ["F.Cu", "In1.Cu"]
- `min_width` (number)
- `nets` (array): Net names, e.g. ["GND", "/USB_D+"]
- `priority` (integer)
- `refill` (boolean)
- `thermal_gap` (number)
- `thermal_spoke` (number)

## board_outline

Mounting holes: hole_inset (mm from the corners, 4 holes of hole_diameter, default 3.2 = M3) and / or holes [[x, y, d], {x, y, diameter, plated, net}]: NPTH, or plated with a ring (e.g. to GND); they become footprints H1, H2 ... out of the BOM and the CPL; one where a part already has a hole is left out. Reports the size, area, the parts now outside or on a cut-out, and warns of holes on parts or tracks and of tracks a cut-out or the outline cuts. One undoable edit. Then zones create for a pour.

Arguments:

- `hole_diameter` (number)
- `hole_inset` (number)
- `holes` (array)

## board_setup

Show or set copper layers and stackup (or a JLCPCB impedance stackup).

The board's layers and stackup. No arguments: what they are (copper layers, thickness, each layer of the stackup). copper_layers (2, 4, 6, ...), thickness (mm, e.g. 1.6), outer_copper_oz / inner_copper_oz (e.g. 1 and 0.5): the stackup is made anew like Board Setup's default. stackup="JLC04161H-7628" (or another of JLCPCB's impedance stackups, 4 and 6 layers, nominal 1.6 mm, though the layers in JLCPCB's tables add up to 1.45-1.65 mm; stackup="list" names them) sets the fab's own: layer count, prepreg / core thicknesses and dielectric constants, for controlled-impedance widths. Fewer layers only when the ones that go are empty. One undoable edit. Pair with set_rules preset (e.g. jlcpcb_4layer) and zones create on the inner layers.

Arguments:

- `copper_layers` (integer)
- `inner_copper_oz` (number)
- `outer_copper_oz` (number)
- `stackup` (string)
- `thickness` (number)

## impedance

Track width for a Z0 / pair for a Zdiff (or the impedance of a width) per layer, from the stackup.

Controlled impedance from the board's stackup, per copper layer; changes nothing (set_rules impedance / diff_impedance writes it into a net class). A layer's reference planes are the nearest plane layers each way: zones over 40% of the board (an unpoured layer: typed power, or empty), not by name; signal layers between are passed over (their copper in H, said). An outer layer is a microstrip over its plane (with the solder mask), an inner one a stripline between two, or an embedded microstrip with a plane on one side; the note lists each layer's coverage. The planes must be poured (zones create) for it to hold. z: a track's width for this Z0 (ohm); z_diff: a pair's width at gap (mm, default 0.2), or its gap at width; neither: the impedance of width (and gap: of the pair). layers: default all. Uses KiCad's transmission line calculations (as pcb_calculator); set board_setup stackup=... first for a fab's dielectrics, and check with the fab's calculator before ordering.

Parameter notes:

- layers: copper layers, e.g. ["F.Cu", "In2.Cu"]; default all

Arguments:

- `gap` (number)
- `layers` (array): default all
- `width` (number)
- `z` (number)
- `z_diff` (number)

## pcb_set_fields

Change parts' fields, value, reference or attributes (DNP, BOM) on the board, as one undoable edit.

Change footprints' fields and attributes on the board as one undoable edit. parts: {"R1": {"Value": "10k", "LCSC": "C25804", "dnp": true}}, or refs [...] with set {...} for many. Value is the value; other names are user fields (added hidden when missing; null removes one). A board made from a schematic takes its fields and references from it: change them there (sch_set_fields), then sync_pcb. Refs that are not found are listed; the other parts are changed anyway.

Arguments:

- `parts` (object)
- `refs` (array): Reference designators, e.g. ["U5", "C12"]
- `set` (object)

## run_drc

Full-board DRC (all KiCad checks), counts by type and the violations.

Full-board DRC (all KiCad checks: clearance, shorts, unconnected, courtyards, silk, edge, holes, ...). Takes seconds on large boards. Output: whole-board counts by type, then (with types/refs/include_warnings=false) the counts matching the filter, then violations (errors first) as 'E|W type itemA[net] x itemB[net] layer @x,y detail'. ## refill_zones

## lcsc_search

Find JLCPCB / LCSC parts by value, package, specs or electrical parameters.

Find JLCPCB / LCSC parts by specification, e.g. value "100nF", package "0402", dielectric "X5R", voltage_min 16, basic_only; or keyword "AMS1117 3.3". Searches KiCadD's parts index (JLCPCB parts in stock, daily data from jlcparts; about 45 MB, downloaded on first use, refreshed weekly), basic parts first, then preferred (no extended fee), then by stock; the first (live, default 1; each is an EasyEDA request, rate-limited with lcsc_fetch) is checked now against LCSC. Each hit lists its electrical parameters when the index has them (MOSFETs, LDOs, op-amps, inductors, diodes: Rds(on), Qg, Ciss, dropout, PSRR, GBW, slew, DCR, Isat, Vf, trr ...; the datasheet's single test condition after @). Filter on them with where {"rds_on": "<=20m", "vds": ">=30", "vgs_th": "1..2.5"}: SI units, prefixes allowed; a part without the parameter does not match (a bad name lists them). Inductor Isat is in the data for about half the inductors and for no basic / preferred one: with where i_sat leave basic_only off (or filter on i_rated). Then sch_add_part lcsc=... places one.

Parameter notes:

- category: resistor, capacitor, inductor, or a JLCPCB category word ("LDO", "MOSFET")
- where: parameter -> condition: "<=20m", ">=30V", "1u..10u", "3.3"

Arguments:

- `basic_only` (boolean)
- `category` (string): resistor, capacitor, inductor, LDO, MOSFET ...
- `dielectric` (string)
- `keyword` (string)
- `limit` (integer)
- `live` (integer)
- `min_stock` (integer)
- `package` (string)
- `tolerance` (number): %, at most
- `update` (boolean): fetch the index again now
- `value` (string): 100nF, 10k, 4k7, 2.2uH
- `voltage_min` (number): V rating, at least
- `where` (object): {param: "<=20m" | ">=30" | "1..2.5"}

## board_render

PNG of the board (layers, zoom to parts or nets, marks): to see silkscreen, crowding, routing.

A picture (PNG) of the board as it is now, to look at what coordinates do not show: silkscreen over pads or other text, part orientation, crowding, how tracks run, what is left unrouted. Rulers in mm on the top and left. size: pixels on the long side (default 1200). labels=true writes every part's reference. path also saves the PNG (relative: in the project folder).

- `zoom` (boolean)

## export_fab

Gerbers, drill, BOM and CPL for JLCPCB, after refill and DRC.

Write the fabrication files for JLCPCB from the board as it is now (unsaved edits included): Gerbers (Protel extensions, X2, job file) and Excellon drill (mm) zipped for the upload, BOM (Comment, Designator, Footprint, LCSC Part #) and CPL (Designator, Mid X, Mid Y, Layer, Rotation) as CSV. Refills the zones first (refill=false skips it), then runs the full DRC and puts its error count first (drc=false skips it). strict=true writes nothing when DRC has errors, a reference is on two footprints or a part is outside the outline. dir defaults to <project>/fab.

Parameter notes:

- strict: refuse to write when DRC has errors or a reference / placement problem is found

Arguments:

- `dir` (string)
- `drc` (boolean): default true
- `refill` (boolean): default true
- `strict` (boolean): write nothing on DRC or placement errors
- `zip` (boolean): default true

## open_board

Open another .kicad_pcb / .kicad_pro on the server (headless only); refused with unsaved changes unless discard.

Open a .kicad_pcb (or .kicad_pro) on the server (kicad-cli api-server), replacing the current project: the board, its schematic when there is one, the project's rules. Reopening the same file reloads it from disk. The agent history starts over. Refused when the open board has unsaved changes, unless discard=true (the refusal lists what board_diff finds changed). Not available with the desktop editor: there the user opens the file in pcbnew.

Parameter notes:

- discard: Drop the open board's unsaved changes (default false)

Arguments:

- `discard` (boolean): drop the open board's unsaved changes

## auto_place

Place parts inside the outline near what they connect to, with optional rules (edges, regions, groups), undoable.

Place parts automatically inside the board outline: each part goes near what it connects to (decoupling caps and pull-ups next to their IC pin), courtyards kept apart, rotations tried, as one undoable edit. Default: the parts outside the board (new from sync_pcb); refs for chosen parts, all for every unlocked part; fixed keeps parts where they are (place connectors first, then lock or fix them). Mounting holes (board_outline's H1..., MountingHole footprints) stay unless refs names them. Functional blocks are kept together: from groups, else the parts' Block field (sch_add_part block); groups "sheets" uses the schematic sheets, false none. Keeping blocks together costs some wire length (group_weight trades one for the other). Bottom side: back lists parts that may go there, back_decaps lets decoupling caps go under their IC pin (SMD only; off by default: two-sided assembly costs more). A dense board: spacing / edge 0 are taken, and silk_room=false keeps room for the courtyards only, as DRC does (no room for references and silk outlines; silk_tidy after); a "no room" says the area per side. Reports wire length before/after, how mixed the blocks are and how far the small passives (decaps, pull-ups) are from their IC pin. Existing tracks and vias are obstacles: on a routed board fewer parts find room, so place before routing (right after sync_pcb), or delete_tracks first. Runs beside the editor (it stays responsive); if the board is changed meanwhile nothing is moved. When every part is already placed (no overlaps, no rules), a result that is not better than the current placement is not applied (force applies it anyway). Moving parts leaves the board's tracks behind and copper zones in their old shape: refill_zones before run_drc.

Parameter notes:

- back: parts that may go to the bottom side, e.g. ["C*", "R12"]
- back_decaps: decoupling caps may go to the bottom, under their IC pin
- force: apply the result even when the current placement is better
- group_weight: pull to the block's centre, 1 = like a two-pin net (default 3)
- groups: {"LDO": ["U2", "C3"], ...} blocks to keep together, "sheets", or false: no blocks
- measure_only: move nothing, report the numbers for the parts as they are
- rules: placement rules; refs may be patterns ("J*"). edge: {"J1": "left"} (left/right/top/bottom/any: the courtyard at that board edge); a connector (USB, RJ45, DC jack: its courtyard well past its pads on one side) is turned to open over that edge, the footprint's own "PCB Edge" line on the edge (else its front flush with it); {"J1": {"side": "left", "overhang": 1}} puts its front 1 mm past the edge, "inside": true keeps it inside like any part; region: {"U1": [x0, y0, x1, y1]} (mm, the courtyard inside); rotation: {"J1": 90}; near: [{"refs": ["Y1", "U1"], "max": 5}] (mm between the centres of their pads). edge/region/rotation are hard, near is reported when it can't be met
- time_limit: seconds for improving the wire length after every part has a legal spot (default 10, -1 no limit); the placement is legal either way

Arguments:

- `all` (boolean)
- `back` (array): parts allowed on the bottom (patterns)
- `back_decaps` (boolean): decoupling caps may go under their IC
- `detail` (boolean): list everything (default: long lists cut short)
- `edge` (number): courtyard to board edge, mm (default 0.5)
- `fixed` (array)
- `force` (boolean): apply even when worse than now
- `group_weight` (number): pull to the block's centre (default 3)
- `groups`: {name: [refs]}, "sheets", or false
- `measure_only` (boolean): report only, move nothing
- `refs` (array)
- `rotate` (boolean): try 90/180/270 (default true)
- `rules` (object): edge / region / rotation / near (tool_help auto_place)
- `silk_room` (boolean): room for silkscreen (default true)
- `spacing` (number): courtyard gap, mm (default 0.25)
- `time_limit` (number): seconds of improving (default 10, -1 none)

## pdn

## reflection
